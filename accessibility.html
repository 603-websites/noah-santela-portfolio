Accessibility

Santella Designs is committed to making this website usable for everyone, including visitors who rely on assistive technologies.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. The site uses semantic HTML and clear headings, supports keyboard navigation, respects your device's reduce-motion setting, provides text alternatives for meaningful images, and keeps color contrast readable.

Accessibility is ongoing work and some areas may still fall short. If you encounter a barrier or have a suggestion, please email Noah at noah@santelladesigns.com and we will work to resolve it promptly.
