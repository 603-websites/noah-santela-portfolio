Privacy Policy

Santella Designs keeps things simple. This page explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and how to reach us about it. Effective date: August 14, 2026.

What we collect

When you use the inquiry forms on this site, we collect your name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to provide it, and your message. That is all. This site has no accounts, takes no payments, and collects nothing else about you.

How it is used

Your inquiry is used for one purpose: so Noah can respond to you. A confirmation may be emailed to you after you send an inquiry.

Who processes it

The inquiry form is processed by Oryx Technologies LLC, our website operator, which delivers your message to Santella Designs by email. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. The typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the fonts load.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Retention and deletion

Inquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and for ordinary business records. If you would like your information deleted, email Noah at noah@santelladesigns.com and we will take care of it.

Questions

For anything about this policy, contact noah@santelladesigns.com.
