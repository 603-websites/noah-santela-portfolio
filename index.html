Repairs &
Restoration

Every piece carries a story. Noah returns well-loved jewelry to the way it deserves to be worn, whether it arrived missing a stone, bent out of shape, or simply tired.

  • Stone setting & replacement for rings and pendants with missing or loose gems
  • Ring resizing to any size necessary
  • Chain repair: broken clasps, links, and jump rings soldered and refinished until they read like new
  • Professional polishing to a mirror finish, with scratches worked out of the metal
  • Watch repair and movement servicing
