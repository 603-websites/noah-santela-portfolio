Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I commission a custom piece?

Send an inquiry through the contact form with a note about what you have in mind. Noah replies within two days to talk through stones, metal, and budget before any work begins.

How long does a custom piece take?

Most commissions take three to six weeks depending on the design, the stones, and the queue at the bench. You get a timeline up front before committing.

What does a custom piece cost?

Every piece is quoted individually based on materials and hours at the bench. Sterling silver work typically starts in the low hundreds; gold and large stones run higher. You approve the quote before work starts.

Do you repair jewelry you did not make?

Yes. Ring resizing, stone resetting, chain and clasp repair, restoration of heirloom pieces, and lapidary recutting are all regular bench work.

Do you ship?

Yes. Finished pieces can be shipped insured within the United States, and inquiries are welcome from anywhere.

Are the original pieces one of a kind?

Yes. Originals are made once and never repeated. When a piece sells, that design is retired.
